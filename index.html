How it all started...

In 2024 I decided to come to Berlin to focus on my work with sound.

Despite producing sound for decades, I have established a career working as a film editor for documentary, fiction and branded content. With a formal education in visual arts, I developed multiple installations and paintings.

This deep understanding of moving images informs my approach to sound.

Not long ago I met Manuel Lima, a classically trained composer, pianist, flutist, and also an inventive sound programmer with an original performance work spanning multiple media.

We decided to team up and are looking for film and video producers willing to break free from generic aesthetics.

With established connections with a multitude of unique instrumentalists, we can produce original music mixing acoustic instruments and electronics, programmed from scratch.

This is how we trip about listening:

Before becoming music, sound is space. It defines the distance between bodies, alters the temperature of a scene, and creates proximity, tension, and memory.

Today, we live surrounded by images. They circulate in excess; they are rapidly constructed, recognized, and forgotten. Sound works differently: it enters the body, organizes time, and restores a physical dimension to the film.

It does not merely accompany a story; it creates the environment where that story can be felt, sometimes as a sense of the uncanny, sometimes as contemplation, and sometimes as a reality that previously had no name.

The sound connects physical and metaphysical dimensions via bodily resonance. Recovering sidelined human elements, it explores connections across existence’s dimensions through language-free trance.

Carioba Ricardo Carioba

I am a visual and sound artist working across multiple mediums. My practice is marked by the questions raised by the concretist tradition in Brazil and its non-representational approach to visual production, as well as by the impact of sound in the brain and its capacity of modulating brainwaves and the vibration of neurons.

My sound performances and installations use light, video, sound and sculpture and aim to build abstract spaces and non-discursive imagery in order to explore the deconstruction of the syntax of our aesthetic experience and to open space to new forms of thinking and relating to the world.

My practice is interested in the problem of visual production as a tool for alienation and social control and in challenging the notions of space, acoustics and architecture, with works that move between the hypnotic and the repulsive and explore situations of physical conflict between space and the spectator.

I held exhibitions in galleries and museums in Brazil, Germany, United Kingdom and Italy, among other countries. As a sound artist, I have performed concerts in São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, New York, Berlin, London, Bordeaux and more.

Manu Manuel Pessoa de Lima

Manuel Pessoa de Lima is a Brazilian composer, sound designer and performer based in Berlin, and co-founder of Carioba Sound. He brings a composer’s ear to audiovisual work, creating scores and sound design for film, dance, theatre and installation.

His soundtrack credits include a National Geographic series, the Brazilian theatre company Hiato, and the sound installation Memories of a Coup. In Berlin, he has also worked as live sound operator for Ligia Lewis’s dance company.

Alongside this, he keeps an experimental practice: three albums, including Realejo, released on vinyl by Black Truffle, and humorous solo performances presented at festivals such as Oscillation, Tectonics, Send-Receive and Schloss Solitude Summerfest.

Classically trained, he holds a doctorate in the Performer-Composer program at CalArts. In every project he listens closely to the space, the story and the people involved, and builds the sound to fit, with a sense of humour about the process.
